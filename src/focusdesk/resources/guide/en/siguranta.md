# Archive, Trash, backup and restore {#arhiva-cos-backup-si-restaurare}
## Purpose {#la-ce-foloseste}
Separate finishing work, organizing history and protecting authoritative files. Rebuildable indexes are not backups.
## Where to find it {#unde-il-gasesti}
Left **Archive**, **Data and backup**, **File**, and selected-task Organize commands.
## Complete, archive or Trash {#finalizare-arhivare-si-cos}
Complete changes status and allows reopening. Archive/unarchive changes visibility separately. Move to Trash uses recoverable operations. **File → Trash… → Restore** keeps UUIDs/descendants; **Delete permanently** is irreversible and confirmed.
## Tutorial K: backup and safe restoration {#tutorial-k-backup-si-restaurare-potrivita}
1. Save important drafts and check the current workspace path.
2. Choose **File → Back up now (full, with files)** or metadata-only with different coverage.
3. Check success and any external copy separately; settings control retention.
4. **Restore backup to a new folder…**, choose an archive.
5. Choose a new/empty folder, separate from the open workspace.
6. Open restored data and verify tasks, descriptions, sessions and managed files before replacing your working copy.
## Saving {#ce-se-salveaza-si-cand}
Backup captures disk state, not drafts. Full backups include managed files, not automatically associated external folders. Restored external paths may need relocating on another PC.
## Common mistakes {#greseli-frecvente}
Same-disk backups do not protect against disk failure. Never test restoration over real data. Use [diagnostics](guide:probleme), not manual deletion of operation files. Retention removes old backups, not active tasks.
