# First steps and workspaces {#primii-pasi-si-workspace-ul}
## Purpose {#la-ce-foloseste}
A workspace holds tasks, descriptions, sessions, history and managed files separately from the executable. Updating FocusDesk must not replace your data folder.
## Where to find it {#unde-il-gasesti}
At startup choose a new, existing or demo workspace. Later use **File → New workspace…**, **Open workspace…** or recent workspaces. The guide also works without a workspace.
## Safe first steps {#pasi-pentru-un-inceput-sigur}
1. Choose **New workspace…**, for example `C:\FocusDeskData`.
2. Confirm creation. New workspaces are empty; **Create demo workspace…** is a separate command.
3. Create a [project/task](guide:organizare).
4. Close and reopen the same workspace; check your task.
5. Plan your [backups](guide:siguranta) before collecting important data.
## Expected result {#ce-trebuie-sa-vezi}
The workspace name appears in the app. Local features need no online account. Read-only access is indicated and mutation commands are disabled.
## Saving {#ce-se-salveaza-si-cand}
**Create** saves a new node immediately. Details and descriptions need **Save/Ctrl+S**. [Meeting notes](guide:intalniri) have separate autosave. Profile preferences are outside the workspace.
## Common mistakes {#greseli-frecvente}
Keep data outside the executable folder, use reasonably short Windows paths and retain backups. A second process may receive read-only access because of the lock; do not force simultaneous writes.
